HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Read once end-to-end (~25 minutes). Then go to page 17 and tear out the cheat card — that's what you'll have next to your monitor while recording. The rest of the document is reference for everything that goes wrong.

THE ONE INSIGHT

The single most important moment in your 2-minute pitch is the second of silence after the words "could be a lie." Most rookies fill that silence with another sentence and immediately weaken the hook. Confident delivery uses silence as punctuation. If you remember nothing else, remember the pause.

ChainTrust · Pitch Playbook2

01 · Why this works

Two things decide whether judges keep watching past second 5:

  1. Pattern interrupt — say something they didn't expect. "We solve a problem" loses every time. "$30 billion. Gone every year. To lies." wins.
  2. Immediate proof — show, don't tell, before skepticism kicks in. Hackathon judges have seen 50 demos that were screenshots. A real signed Solana transaction in the first minute resets every expectation.

This is what every winning pitch I studied has in common — Airbnb's seed deck, Stripe's "seven lines of code", Brex's YC Demo Day, Steve Jobs's iPhone keynote, Anatoly's early Solana talks, and the last 5 ETHGlobal Demo Day winners. Demo-driven, not deck-driven. Concrete numbers, not adjectives. Show before tell.

02 · The 30-second hook

A hook works only if you believe it when you say it. Read all three out loud. Pick the one that doesn't feel like a lie. Then own it.

Hook A — "Lying" RECOMMENDED

Pattern interrupt + specific dollar figure. Sets up the demo as proof. The boldest opener — judges will see 200+ pitches; this one gets remembered.

FACE CAM. Look directly at lens. No music. Slight pause before speaking.
"Every metric in a startup pitch deck could be a lie."
(Beat — 1 second silence. Let it land.)
"VCs lose thirty billion dollars a year backing founders who fudged the numbers. So we built ChainTrust — to make lying mathematically impossible."

Hook B — "Watch this"

Most demo-forward. Zero exposition, all proof. Use this if your devnet RPC is fast that day. Risk: an 8-second slow confirm reads as dead air.

Cut to: testnet-demo screen, wallet pre-connected.
"This is a real Solana transaction." (Click — Anchor proof.) "It cost a quarter of a cent. It just made a startup metric impossible to fake." (Click Explorer link, transaction shows confirmed.) "Most pitches are PowerPoints. ChainTrust is proof."

Hook C — "Trust me"

Most empathetic. Use if delivering live to investors who are themselves VCs — meets them where they are.

FACE CAM.
"Investors hear 'trust me' from founders thirty thousand times a year. ChainTrust makes 'trust me' obsolete — we replace it with mathematical proof, on Solana, in under two seconds. Let me show you."
⚠ AVOID

"Hi, I'm Uros, CEO of ChainTrust" — same as 199 other pitches. Names come at 1:30. Lead with the bold claim.

03 · The full 2-minute script

Speaking pace target: ~150 words per minute (~300 words total). The script below is ~285 words; the deliberate pause adds ~15 seconds of useful silence.

0:00 – 0:30 Hook + setup 30s
📺 Face cam, full frame. No motion graphics yet. 🎙 You, looking at lens.
"Every metric in a startup pitch deck could be a lie.

(Beat — 1 second of silence.)

VCs lose thirty billion dollars a year backing founders who fudged the numbers.

(Cut to: testnet-demo page on screen, wallet already connected to Devnet.)

So we built ChainTrust — to make lying mathematically impossible. Watch."
0:30 – 1:00 The demo (most important 30 seconds) 30s
📺 testnet-demo page. Cursor visible. Move with intent. 🎙 Speak only while clicking.
"I'm signing a startup metric on Solana right now."

(Click "Anchor proof on-chain". Wallet popup. Click sign.)

"One signature. Two seconds. A quarter of a cent in fees."

(Tx confirms — toast shows up. Click "View on Solana Explorer".)

"And there it is — on the actual Solana network. Forever. Any investor can verify it. Nobody can fake it. That's the entire ChainTrust thesis: replace trust-me with proof."
⏱ SILENCE IS YOUR FRIEND

During the 2-second tx confirmation, do not narrate. Let the wallet talk. Judges can read "Confirmed" themselves. Talking over the demo halves its impact.

04 · Delivery

The script is half. Delivery is the other half. These are not optional.

🎙 Voice

BeatTone
HookSlow. ~120 wpm. Each word lands. Drop pitch on "lie." Long pause after the $30B figure.
DemoMatch the action. Speak only while clicking. Silence during the 2-second confirm.
Diff.Speed up. ~165 wpm. List items in clean rhythm.
TeamSlow again. Sound proud. Let your name land.
CloseConversational, calm. Like you've already won.

🚫 No filler words

Cut these in rehearsal. Replace with silence. Silence reads as authority.

  • like
  • um
  • you know
  • kind of
  • basically
  • so yeah

🧍 Body

  • Stand still. No pacing. Pacing reads as nervous. Plant feet shoulder-width apart.
  • Hands visible. Not in pockets. Not crossed. One gesture per major sentence.
  • Slight forward lean. Reads as "engaged."
  • Eye contact with the lens (not the screen — the lens). On stage: scan three judges in three sentences.
  • Smile only at traction stats and the close. Never during the problem.

👕 What to wear

  • One solid colour above the chest — black, dark blue, or charcoal. Not white (bright on camera).
  • No logos (any logo not yours competes).
  • No patterns or stripes (moiré on video).

The two pauses that matter most

  1. The pause before "lie." One full second of silence. The most important second in the pitch. It says: I am confident enough to wait.
  2. The pause after the Explorer click. Let the judges see "Confirmed" on screen. Don't talk over it.

06 · Pre-recording checklist

Run through this in order. Don't skip steps — every line is a real failure mode I've seen.

🪪 Identity & environment

  • Phantom wallet pre-funded with 1 SOL on Devnet (so the airdrop step is unnecessary or instant)
  • Devnet RPC tested 30 minutes before recording — sometimes the first request of the day is slow
  • System time in sync (some Solana operations care)

🖥 Browser

  • Browser zoom at 110% so cursor and tx hash are readable on small screens
  • All other tabs closed (no Slack notifications popping up)
  • Bookmarks bar hidden (Ctrl+Shift+B)
  • System notifications disabled — Mac: Do Not Disturb. Windows: Focus Assist.
  • Browser cache cleared if you've been testing recently (avoid stale state)

🎬 Recording

  • Resolution: 1920×1080 minimum
  • Audio: external mic if you have one. Phone earbuds beat laptop mic.
  • Background: clean wall or office. Tidy what's behind you.
  • Lighting: face the window, don't sit with it behind you
  • One solid colour shirt above chest (no white, no logos, no patterns)

📋 Tabs to pre-stage

  • Tab 1: localhost:8080/testnet-demo — wallet pre-connected
  • Tab 2: localhost:8080/dashboard — signed in as investor
  • Tab 3: localhost:8080/screener — Tideland RWA detail open
  • Tab 4: github.com/urosradojicic/ChainTrust-SOL/blob/master/judges/01-why-we-win.md
  • Tab 5: End-card image (or use OBS scene)

🧪 Final dry-run

  • One full take recorded as warmup, watched, discarded
  • One full take recorded as keeper, not watched until next day
  • Time the keeper take — must be ≤ 1:55 (5s buffer for nerves)

07 · Common mistakes

These are mistakes I've seen kill otherwise-strong hackathon pitches. Each one is specific to the kind of pitch you're giving.

MistakeWhy it loses / What to do instead
Opening with "Hi I'm Uros, CEO"Same as 199 other pitches. Lead with the bold claim. Names come at 1:30.
Reading bullet points off slidesJudges check phones. Slides ≤ 4 words each. Voiceover does the talking.
Saying "We're disrupting fundraising"Empty corporate adjective. Say exactly what you do. "We make startup metrics non-repudiable on Solana" is specific and unfakeable.
Talking over the demoJudges can't process two streams. Click → silence → react. The product talks.
Buzzwords without proofVapor signal. Every "AI", "DAO", "ZK proof" needs a file in the repo backing it. We have those files. Reference them.
Apologising for what's missingConfidence killer. Don't say "we know we still need to do X." Say what works. The deferred list lives in the repo for those who care.
Going over 2 minutesDisqualification at Colosseum. Rehearse with stopwatch. Trim until 1:55.

Showing code on screenNobody reads code in 5 seconds. Show artifacts (UI, Explorer, GitHub).
Saying "we're the first to do X"Almost never true. One judge will know the counter-example. Say "we make X verifiable" instead.
Speed-talking to fit 2 minutesAudible nervousness. Cut content instead of rushing it. Pacing matters more than completeness.
Ending with "Thanks for listening"Soft. The pitch ends with the ASK, not gratitude. "Vote for us if you agree" is the last line.

08 · Backup script — when Devnet stalls

Devnet is shared infrastructure. It's been known to take 8–15 seconds on the first request of the day. Eight seconds of silence on camera is a disaster. Never narrate the slowness. Pivot.

The 5-second test

If by 5 seconds after clicking "Anchor proof" you don't see the wallet popup, switch to plan B. Decide quickly. Hesitation reads worse than the failure itself.

Plan B — pivot to a previous tx

Open Phantom's transaction history (or have a Solana Explorer tab open with one of your previous test transactions). The pivot voiceover:

As the wallet stalls, smoothly mouse over to your transaction history.
"Devnet is busy right now — but here's the same proof from yesterday." (Click into a previous tx on Solana Explorer.) "Same flow. Same fee. Same chain. Every metric we publish has a permanent on-chain receipt — verifiable forever."

This actually strengthens the pitch. You demonstrate a real signed transaction and show that the system has history. Judges who notice the pivot will respect the recovery.

Plan C — pure narration with pre-recorded clip

If you're paranoid: pre-record the testnet demo flow once when devnet is working, save the screen clip, and have it ready. If live demo fails, narrate over the pre-recorded clip. Less powerful than live, but better than dead air.

What NOT to say if it stalls

  • ❌ "Sorry, devnet seems slow today"
  • ❌ "Oh wait, let me try that again"
  • ❌ "Hmm, that's weird"
  • ❌ Any silence longer than 3 seconds

Pre-flight: make it less likely

  • Run the demo flow 30 minutes before recording. Devnet's first request is the slowest.
  • Pre-fund the wallet with 1 SOL so airdrop step isn't needed
  • Use a paid RPC if you have one (Helius free tier works) — usually faster than the default
  • Have Plan B tab open and ready before you start recording

09 · Q&A — likely judge questions

If the format includes Q&A after the pitch, prepare for these. Each answer is ≤30 seconds.

Likely questions, ranked by frequency

Q1 · BUSINESS MODEL

"How do you make money?"

Don't say "we're focused on adoption first." That kills VC interest.

Your answer (~25s): "Two revenue streams. First: founders pay a small per-anchor fee — sub-cent on Solana, but at scale that's meaningful. Second: investors subscribe to our LP-grade due-diligence stack — the screener, the 14-tab panel, the PDF reports. Bloomberg-for-Solana-startups. We're aiming for $200/month per investor seat."

Q2 · MOAT

"What stops a competitor from copying you?"

Your answer (~25s): "Three things. One: network effect — every founder anchored is one more proof investors search through us. Two: oracle relationships — independent attesters take time to build trust with. Three: the audit trail itself — once a startup has 12 months of on-chain history with us, switching is expensive. The chain is the moat."

Q3 · WHY SOLANA

"Why Solana over Ethereum or an L2?"

Your answer (~20s): "Cost and finality. At a quarter of a cent per anchor, founders publish updates monthly without thinking. On L2s it's 50× more expensive — adoption dies at that price. Two-second confirmation makes the live-demo flow possible. ADR-0002 in our repo has the full reasoning."

Q4 · ADVERSARIAL

"What stops a startup from publishing fake metrics on-chain anyway?"

Your answer (~25s): "Nothing — the chain doesn't validate the truth, only the timestamp and the signature. That's why we have independent oracle attestation as a separate layer. Founders publish; oracles verify against bank statements, GitHub commits, and on-chain TVL; investors see both. We're not magic. We're provable accountability."

Q5 · TRACTION

"Who's using this today?"

Your answer (~15s): "Pre-launch. We built the trust layer first because adoption follows credibility, not the other way around. Our beta target is post-Frontier with 10 design-partner startups."

If you have any traction, lead with it instead. "Three startups have signed up to be design partners" is enough.

Q6 · SECURITY

"You said three audits — which firms? When?"

Your answer (~15s): "OtterSec, Sec3, and CertiK — all in April. Zero critical findings. Plus an internal adversarial deep audit in May that closed sixty more findings. Reports are public in the docs/internal/security-audit folder."

Q7 · TEAM

"Has any of you built a startup before?"

Answer honestly. If no: lean into "young + technical + audited" framing.

Q8 · ASK

"What do you actually want from us?"

Your answer (~15s): "Frontier prize money lets us run six months. Beyond that: we'd love three intros — one to a Solana-focused fund, one to an existing founder who'd be a design partner, one to a security firm we can deepen the audit cadence with."

11 · The 7-day practice plan

This is non-negotiable. The difference between a good pitch and a winning pitch is rehearsal volume.

DayPracticeTime
Day 7Read the script aloud 5 times. Don't memorise — internalise. Time each read.~25 min
Day 6Record yourself on phone. Watch it. Note the 3 worst moments.~30 min
Day 5Fix those 3 moments. Re-record. Time it. Trim to 1:55.~45 min
Day 4Rehearse with the screen, not just voice. Practise the cursor moves.~30 min
Day 3Rehearse with one teammate watching live. Get one criticism. Fix it.~30 min
Day 2Final dress rehearsal — full clothes, full background, full setup. Record once. Don't watch yet.~30 min
Day 1Watch yesterday's take. Pick the one moment that still feels off. Practise just that moment 10 times.~20 min
Day 0Two warm-up takes. Discard. Then record the keeper. Don't watch it. Send it. Sleep.~30 min
🛑 THE "DON'T WATCH THE FINAL TAKE" RULE

You'll always find something to fix. The judge won't care about the thing you'd fix. Send it. Watching the final take before submitting is how good pitches die in re-recording loops at 2am the night before submission. Trust the rehearsal. Send the keeper.

How to give yourself feedback

When you watch your phone-recorded takes, look for three things only:

  1. Energy — does it feel alive, or read off a script?
  2. Pace — any moment you rushed? Any moment that dragged?
  3. Clarity — is there a single sentence a viewer would have to rewind for?

Don't critique your face, voice, or "did I sound silly." Those are not actionable.

Vocabulary — words to use, words to avoid

✅ USE

  • Trust layer — what we are
  • Cryptographic proof — what we provide
  • Non-repudiable — once posted, can't be denied
  • Production-grade — not "production-ready"
  • Audited — past tense, three times
  • Verifiable forever — the on-chain commitment
  • Anchor — the verb we use for "publish on-chain"
  • "Replace 'trust me' with proof" — the thesis line

❌ AVOID

  • "Disrupting" / "revolutionising" — empty
  • "Web3 native" — meaningless
  • "Game-changer" — corporate cliché
  • "AI-powered" (unless we mean it specifically)
  • "Easy" — never sounds true
  • "Solution" — vague; say what we do
  • "Passionate" — every founder says this
  • "Cutting-edge" — judges roll eyes

Tone

  • Confident not arrogant. We've shipped, we've been audited, we know what we are.
  • Specific not vague. Numbers. File paths. Audit firm names.
  • Empathetic to the investor. They've been burned. We're the fix.
  • Direct not corporate. Short sentences win. No "leveraging synergies."

The two anchor sentences

If anyone asks "what is ChainTrust" outside the pitch, use one of these:

"ChainTrust is the trust layer for Solana startup fundraising. Founders anchor metrics on-chain, oracles verify them, investors get cryptographic proof instead of self-reported decks."
"We make startup metrics non-repudiable on Solana. Same flow as Stripe for payments — but for trust."
